Planning Your Visit

Easy to Find & Park

Located in the heart of the Animal District, our hospital is easy to reach by car, bus, or bike — and there's always free parking on-site.

By Car

Take exit 12 off the Pawnee Highway. Free parking on-site — 40 spaces including 6 oversized for large vehicles.

By Public Transit

Bus routes 14, 27, and 42 stop directly in front of the clinic. The Animal District light rail station is a 4-minute walk away.

Accessibility

Step-free entrance, accessible restrooms, wide exam room doors, and extra-large tables for giant breeds and wheelchair access.
