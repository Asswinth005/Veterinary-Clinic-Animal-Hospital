Meet Our Founder

★ 20+ Years
Founder & Chief Veterinarian

Dr. Maya Collins

DVM, Fear-Free Certified · 20+ yrs

Dr. Collins founded PawCare in 2005 with a radical idea: veterinary care could be both clinically excellent and genuinely kind. Two decades later, she still personally mentors every new veterinarian who joins the team and leads our Fear-Free training program. Her special interests include soft-tissue surgery, senior pet wellness, and reducing anxiety in veterinary visits.

Fear-Free is a certification program that trains veterinary professionals to reduce stress and anxiety in pets during visits. It involves gentle handling, calming pheromones, minimal restraint, and positive reinforcement with treats. All of our vets and technicians are Fear-Free certified.

It depends on your pet's needs. For routine wellness, any of our general practitioners are excellent. For specific concerns (surgery, internal medicine, dentistry), we'll match you with the appropriate specialist. Call us and we'll help you choose.

Yes. We welcome second opinions and will thoroughly review your pet's history, diagnostics, and current treatment plan. Our goal is always your pet's best outcome, even if that means confirming another vet's approach.
