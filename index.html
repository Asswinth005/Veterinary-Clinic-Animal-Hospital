Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our safety standards, appointment prep, and compassionate care.

Bring any previous medical records and a fresh stool sample if possible. Keep your pet on a leash or in a carrier. If your pet gets anxious in new places, let us know — we offer pre-visit calming protocols and Fear-Free handling techniques. Arrive 10 minutes early to fill out paperwork.

Core vaccines for dogs include rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and adenovirus. For cats: rabies, FVRCP, and FeLV (for kittens and at-risk adults). Lifestyle vaccines (like Bordetella or Lyme) are recommended based on exposure risk. We'll create a personalized schedule during your wellness exam.

Every surgical patient receives a pre-anesthetic blood panel, dedicated IV fluids, multi-parameter monitoring (ECG, blood pressure, oxygen, temperature), and a dedicated veterinary nurse. We use advanced pain management protocols including local blocks to minimize discomfort. Post-op recovery is closely supervised until your pet is awake and stable.

Yes! Our entire team is Fear-Free certified. That means we prioritize gentle handling, minimal restraint, calming pheromones, and treat-based rewards. We also offer pre-visit pharmaceuticals for extremely anxious pets when appropriate. Your pet's emotional well-being is just as important as their physical health.

We accept all major credit cards, CareCredit, and Scratchpay for flexible financing. Our wellness membership plan spreads preventive care costs into affordable monthly payments. Our front desk team is happy to help you submit pet insurance claims and will provide itemized invoices for reimbursement.
