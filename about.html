Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

2005

PawCare Opens Its Doors

Dr. Maya Collins founded PawCare with a simple vision: veterinary care that feels like family. The first clinic opened with just three team members and a whole lot of heart.

2010

AAHA Accreditation

We earned accreditation from the American Animal Hospital Association — a mark of excellence achieved by only 12% of veterinary practices in North America.

2015

State-of-the-Art Facility

We moved into our custom-designed hospital with digital imaging, surgical suites, and separate dog and cat waiting areas to reduce stress.

2018

Fear-Free Certification

Our entire team became Fear-Free certified, transforming how we handle anxious pets with pheromones, gentle techniques, and positive reinforcement.

2026

12,000+ Pets Strong

Today, PawCare is a full-service animal hospital with 25+ specialists, 24/7 emergency care, and a community of pet parents who trust us with their family members.
