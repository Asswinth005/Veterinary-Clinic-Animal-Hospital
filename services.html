Signature Services

Deep Dive Into Key Care Areas

A closer look at the specialties our team is most passionate about.

Surgical Suite
Surgical Excellence

Advanced Surgery & Anesthesia

Our surgical suite features multi-parameter monitoring (ECG, blood pressure, oxygen, temperature), dedicated IV fluids, and a veterinary nurse assigned to every patient. We use advanced pain management including local nerve blocks for comfort before, during, and after procedures.

  • Pre-anesthetic blood work included
  • Dedicated anesthetist monitoring
  • Heated surgery table & warm air blanket
Schedule Consult $349+
Dental Suite
Dental Health

Dentistry & Oral Surgery

By age 3, over 80% of dogs and 70% of cats show signs of dental disease. Our dental suite provides ultrasonic scaling, polishing, digital dental X-rays, and extractions — all under safe anesthesia with full monitoring. We also provide home dental care kits and coaching.

  • Full-mouth dental X-rays
  • Ultrasonic scaling & polishing
  • Home care kit included
Book Dental $229
Imaging Suite
Diagnostics

Advanced Imaging & Lab Work

Our in-house laboratory delivers blood chemistry, CBC, cytology, and urinalysis results in under 30 minutes. Digital radiography and ultrasound allow us to see inside without delay — critical for urgent cases and accurate diagnoses.

  • Digital X-ray & ultrasound
  • In-house bloodwork in 30 minutes
  • Same-day results & treatment plan

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our services, pricing, and what to expect.

Start with a wellness exam. Our veterinarian will assess your pet's overall health, discuss any concerns, and recommend the appropriate services. We'll never push unnecessary treatments — we explain the "why" behind every recommendation.

Listed prices are starting points for standard cases. Final cost depends on your pet's size, age, health status, and any complications. We always provide a written estimate before treatment and get your approval before proceeding.

Yes, for life-threatening emergencies we accept walk-ins during opening hours. Call ahead so we can prepare. After hours, our phone triage directs you to our 24/7 emergency partner hospital. Never wait if your pet is in distress.

Absolutely. We welcome second opinions and will review your pet's full history, previous diagnostics, and treatment plan. Our goal is your pet's best outcome — even if that means confirming another vet's approach.
